About us

Design journalism for the thoughtful home

Evolving Spaces Daily is an independent publication covering interior design, architecture, and the ideas that shape how we live. We publish practical, researched articles — no fluff, no trends for trends' sake.

Our mission

We believe that good design is accessible, not exclusive. Every article we publish aims to demystify the built environment — from the physics of passive heating to the psychology of negative space. We work with architects, designers, and journalists who share our commitment to clarity and rigour.

Founded in 2021, Evolving Spaces Daily has grown into a trusted resource for homeowners, design professionals, and anyone who wants to understand the spaces they inhabit.

“We don't chase trends. We explain the principles behind them — and when a trend is worth following.”

What guides us

Our editorial values are simple: specificity over vagueness, evidence over opinion, and design that serves people and planet.

  • Rigour — every claim is checked, every material compared.
  • Clarity — we write for curious non-experts and seasoned professionals alike.
  • Responsibility — we highlight sustainable materials, passive strategies, and long-life design.
  • Independence — no sponsored content disguised as editorial. Ever.

Editorial team

A small, dedicated group of writers, designers, and researchers. We also work with a network of contributing architects and journalists.

Contributors

We work with a carefully selected group of freelance journalists, architects, and designers. Regular contributors include:

  • Sofia Lindqvist — architecture, passive house, Nordic design
  • Aisha Rahman — small spaces, storage, interior styling
  • James Okonkwo — urbanism, adaptive reuse, community housing
  • Lena Fischer — color theory, textiles, material culture

Editorial independence

Evolving Spaces Daily is reader-supported. We do not accept payment for coverage, and we clearly label any sponsored content (which is rare). Our recommendations are based on research and firsthand testing — never on advertising relationships.

We are a member of the Independent Design Media Alliance and adhere to its code of ethics.
