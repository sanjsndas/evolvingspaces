Before you write

I want to pitch an article. What should I include? +
Send a short summary (2–3 paragraphs) outlining the angle, why it fits our audience, and any relevant links to previous work. Please review our About page for tone and style first.
Do you accept sponsored posts? +
We are editorially independent and do not publish sponsored content. We do accept advertising partnerships that are clearly labelled and separated from editorial.
Can I republish an article from Evolving Spaces Daily? +
Yes, with permission. We typically allow syndication with full attribution and a link back to the original. Please contact us with the details of your request.
I have a correction or update for a published piece. +
We value accuracy. Please email us with the article title, URL, and the specific correction. We’ll review and update as needed.
