Cookies Policy

Last updated: 15 March 2025

This policy explains how Evolving Spaces Daily uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

In short: We use essential cookies to make the site work and optional analytics cookies to understand how it's used. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, improve user experience, and provide information to site owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by other domains we use for services like analytics).

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for the website to function properly, such as remembering your cookie preferences and enabling basic navigation.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with the site by collecting anonymised information about pages visited, time spent, and any errors encountered. This helps us improve our content and user experience.
  • Functionality cookies: Allow the site to remember choices you make (such as your language or region) to provide a more personalised experience. These are optional and disabled by default unless you opt in.

We do not use advertising or targeting cookies on this site. We also do not sell or share your cookie data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Specific cookies we use

Below is a detailed list of the cookies we currently use, their purpose, and their duration.

Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
es_cookie_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent preferences. 1 year
es_session_id Essential Maintains your session while navigating the site (e.g., for form submissions). Session
_ga Analytics (third-party) Used by Google Analytics to distinguish unique users and track site usage. Anonymised IP. 2 years
_gid Analytics (third-party) Used by Google Analytics to distinguish users for a shorter period. 24 hours
es_preferences Functionality Remembers your display preferences (e.g., dark mode, if available). 6 months

Note: Third-party cookies are subject to the respective privacy policies of their providers (e.g., Google Analytics). We do not control these cookies, but we have configured them to anonymise IP addresses where possible.

4. Managing your cookie preferences

When you first visit our site, you will see a cookie banner where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can also manage cookies directly through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

Please note that blocking some cookies may affect the functionality of the site. Essential cookies cannot be disabled through our consent tool as they are necessary for the site to operate.

5. Do Not Track signals

Some browsers support "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals. Our site does not currently respond to DNT signals because there is no industry standard for compliance. However, we do not track users across third-party websites for advertising purposes, and we honour cookie consent choices as described above.

6. Third-party services

We use a limited number of third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Google Analytics: For measuring site traffic and understanding user behaviour. We have enabled IP anonymisation and do not allow Google to use your data for its own purposes.
  • Email newsletter service: If you subscribe, our email provider may use cookies to track email opens and clicks. This is governed by their privacy policy and only applies after you opt in.

We do not embed social media pixels (e.g., Facebook, Twitter) or advertising trackers on our site.

7. Changes to this Cookies Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
